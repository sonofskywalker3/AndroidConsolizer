Seed Planting Ghost Fix — one bug fix since v3.8.4.

Fixed:
- Crop seeds no longer show a translucent "ghost" preview in front of your character while planting. The single-tile placement ghost added in v3.8 for machines and sprinklers was also catching seed packets, which plant directly into tilled soil and don't need a placement preview. Seeds now plant cleanly with no ghost; furniture and machine/sprinkler placement ghosts are unchanged.
